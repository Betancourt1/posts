I've been watching Mr Robot for umpteenth time and just finished the forth episode of season 01 called da3m0ns.mp4; great episode where we see how Elliot goes through a withdrawal episode due to his recent drug adiction to morphine. While this episode he's dragged inside his head and is being confronted time and again with the very same question: **who's your monster?** The episode itself doesn't provide any anwser to that question besides a very subtle hints -or at least that we can catch at this serie's point, but if you're rewatching it you can understand the hints- and the editing is quite esoteric and introspective. All the hints that we have about what does it mean when they ask Elliot who's their monster are filled with references to his past, his childhood trauma, his hate for e-corp and his loneliness but, in the end, we doesn't know for sure if any of this things are _their_ monster. 
The name of the episode also give us a hint about what the Elliot's monster is. "da3m0n" refers to a _daemon_ which in informatics is a process running in the background of a computer's systems; is also a play in words with _demon_ who refers to... well, demons. xd 

## but really
This episode always leaves me with the sensation that the question asked to Elliot is also being asked to me: who's your monster? And I, just like Elliot, have no idea. I've been thinking a lot about what the hell could it mean that something could be **my monster** at all. Maybe my monster is the thing I fear to confront inside me? Maybe is the bad things that have happened to me? Myself? Later in the series, we find that our protagonist isn't Elliot but a new identity called The Mastermind and, ironically, he's the Elliot's monster. Elliot creates The Mastermind to fight the things he feared the most and have hurt him in the past.
