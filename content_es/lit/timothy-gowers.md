# Timothy Gowers — *Thoughts about the Leiden Declaration*

> If we arrive at a world where mathematical theorems are no longer associated with mathematicians, maybe that won’t be any more problematic than the fact that stars aren’t named after astronomers and most aren’t named at all.

> Indeed, if the current system becomes unsustainable because of a flood of AI-generated and AI-aided content, I would regard that as a beneficial consequence of AI.
