# Mark Fisher — *Capitalist Realism*

> Time after time, the villain in Hollywood films will return out to be the 'evil corporation'. Far from undermining capitalist realism, this gestural anti-capitalism actually reinforces it.

> There is a sense in which it simply is the case that the political elite are our servants; the miserable service they provide from us is to launder our libidos, to obligingly re-present for us our disavowed desires as if they had nothing to do with us.

> The fantasy being that western consumerism, far from being instrinsically implicated in systemic global inequality, could itserlf solve them. All we have to do is buy the right products.
